COMMUNITY CODE

Play fair.

These rules can be customized before launch.

01 — Respect everyone

No harassment, hate speech, threats or targeted bullying.

02 — No cheating

No hacked clients, unfair exploits or unauthorized advantages.

03 — No griefing

Respect other players, their builds and their property.

04 — No spam

Don't flood chat, advertise unrelated servers or disrupt the community.

05 — Follow staff instructions

Staff decisions and server-specific rules must be respected.

06 — Use common sense

If something clearly harms the community, don't do it.
